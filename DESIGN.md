# Design

## Theme

Warm coffee-brand palette with a light menu surface and a dark espresso signature area.

## Style Direction

Minimal, mobile-first food ordering interface. Clear hierarchy, few visible controls, and product photography used only where it helps recognition.

## Color Direction

Keep espresso, crema, milk, and citrus brand colors. Use berry only for order-state emphasis; do not introduce extra accents or decorative gradients.

## Density

Concise mobile ordering density: key offers first, category navigation second, full menu and prices in a dedicated catalog view.

## Typography

One confident sans-serif family with strong size contrast for prices and concise headings. Avoid decorative uppercase tracking outside small labels.

## Motion

Crisp, functional transitions for category changes, cards entering view, and basket feedback. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Components

Compact header, offer strip, category filters, product cards, persistent mobile basket action, and a clear order summary.

## BeUniq Rule Priorities

Clarity before decoration, restrained radius and shadow use, no fake social proof, no generic marketing copy, and no visually competing calls to action.

## Visual Review Needed

Check the first mobile viewport, category navigation, basket access, image crops, and price contrast on a real browser before release.
