# Product

GO GO COFFEE is a digital menu for a local coffee and fast-food point.

## Audience

Mobile customers who want to choose food and place a WhatsApp order quickly.

## Primary Design Goal

Make the menu, prices, basket, and order path immediately understandable on a phone.

## Voice And Copy Rules

Short, friendly, concrete food and ordering language. Keep product names, descriptions, and prices factual. Avoid vague premium claims and duplicate calls to action.

## References

Warm, compact cafe ordering flow: discover an offer, choose items, review basket, order.

## Constraints

Preserve every menu category, item, price, basket behavior, and WhatsApp ordering flow. Prioritize mobile use without sacrificing desktop browsing.

## Do Not Change

GO GO COFFEE name, warm espresso-crema-citrus palette, current menu data, prices, Instagram link, and WhatsApp number.
